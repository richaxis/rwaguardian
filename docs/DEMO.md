# Hackathon demo

1. Open the deployed Vercel app.
2. Connect the wallet.
3. Confirm the RWA asset is loaded from the contract.
4. Click **Run GenLayer verification**.
5. Confirm the transaction.
6. Wait for the GenLayer result.
7. Show the updated score and verification status.

The important demo point is that the verification decision is made by
the Intelligent Contract, not by a frontend API.
